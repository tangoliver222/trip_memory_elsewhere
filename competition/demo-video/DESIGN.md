# Elsewhere video design reference

## Overview

Elsewhere is a mobile-first private travel-memory space presented inside a deep
black field. The interface combines a persistent Three.js point-cloud world,
recognizable original media and sparse, editorial typography. Layouts are
spatial rather than dashboard-like: fragments become anchors in one continuous
memory scene while functional controls stay restrained and legible.

## Colors

- **Deep field**: `#040506` — primary background and negative space.
- **Secondary field**: `#07090B` — layered scene depth.
- **Raised surface**: `#0C1013` — restrained controls and overlays.
- **Primary text / particles**: `#EEF0ED` — headings, active particles and focus.
- **Secondary text**: `#CDD2CF` — factual support copy.
- **Muted text**: `#959B9F` — metadata and inactive controls.
- **Quiet text**: `#62696E` — distant or de-emphasized data.
- **Human warmth**: `#B8AD9B` — rare relationship and confirmation accents.
- **Uncertain**: `#84959B` — unresolved or incomplete evidence.
- **Conflict**: `#9C7B6D` — conflicting evidence only.

## Typography

- **Editorial serif**: Georgia 300/400. Brand name, discoveries and memory
  statements; compact line-height and careful negative tracking.
- **Functional sans**: Inter 400. Navigation, controls, status and metadata.
- Major video chapter lines may use 52–72px Georgia in landscape; interface
  text remains faithful to the captured mobile UI.
- Small labels use generous tracking and the muted gray hierarchy rather than
  bright color.

## Elevation

Depth comes from spatial scale, particle density, camera distance and original
media overlap. Raised UI uses `#0C1013`, thin `#E6EAE7` borders at roughly 12%
opacity and restrained blur. Avoid generic glossy cards and dramatic drop
shadows; local bloom belongs to semantic particle focus points only.

## Components

- **Memory Globe**: point-cloud earth with geographic city anchors.
- **City Fragment Cohorts**: original photos, receipts and text arranged around
  data-derived cluster centers.
- **Fragment Field**: searchable spatial database with focus and depth states.
- **Fragment Lens**: an original extracted from its spatial position into a
  readable, source-first overlay.
- **Discovery Growth**: three dated originals connect before the shared entity
  and conclusion appear.
- **Else Orb / Answer Layer**: the same ambient identity moves into a sourced
  answer sheet; it is not a duplicated chat avatar.
- **Bottom Navigation**: quiet three-way product navigation with one active
  capsule.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do's

- Keep original images identifiable and naturally colored at low saturation.
- Use particles to form, connect, focus and explain actual data objects.
- Let each chapter move through the same spatial world with calm camera motion.
- Keep factual evidence and Google processing provenance readable.
- Use warm gray only for confirmed relationships or active human decisions.

### Don'ts

- Do not add blue-purple neon, gold luxury styling or random stars.
- Do not use generic cards as the main visual structure.
- Do not animate every layer simultaneously.
- Do not reveal a discovery conclusion before its evidence.
- Do not imply Gemini analyzed data that was handled deterministically.
