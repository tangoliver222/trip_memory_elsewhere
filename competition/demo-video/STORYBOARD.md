# Elsewhere competition demo storyboard

**Format:** 1920×1080, landscape, 30 fps
**Target duration:** 4:38
**Audio:** Mandarin voiceover + low sustained ambient pad + restrained spatial SFX
**VO direction:** calm Chinese voice, factual and intimate; deliberate pauses, no advertising cadence
**Style basis:** `DESIGN.md`

## Asset audit

| Asset | Type | Beat | Role |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `capture/screenshots/scroll-000.png` | Full-page capture | 1, 8 | Public product opener and closing proof |
| `capture/assets/earth-topology.png` | Texture | 1, 4 | World depth and geographic structure |

| `capture/assets/favicon.svg` | Brand mark | 1, 8 | Opening and closing identity |

All captured assets are used. The actual browser recording remains the primary
evidence; motion-design layers explain rather than replace it.

## Beat 1 — The scattered archive (0.00–33.15s)

**Concept:** Begin inside darkness with only several recognizable original
fragments at different depths. They do not become a collage; thin point trails
pull them into the outline of Elsewhere's world. The viewer understands the
problem before the brand appears.

**VO:** Beat 1 from `SCRIPT.md`.

**Visual:** `earth-topology.png` is a barely visible deep layer. The Bangkok
photo and two accepted original frames float in CSS 3D. A deterministic Canvas
particle field condenses behind them. “不是旅行相册” and “私人记忆世界” enter
word by word. The small favicon resolves into the Elsewhere wordmark.

**Techniques:** Canvas procedural particles; CSS 3D fragment drift; per-word
kinetic typography; SVG path drawing between originals.

**Transition:** cinematic zoom through the newly formed globe into the upload
screen, 0.6s.

**Depth:** BG topology and sparse dust; MG originals and globe; FG title.

**SFX:** distant room tone, three soft archival paper taps, low condensation
tone on globe formation.

## Beat 2 — Real files enter (33.15–75.52s)

**Concept:** The aesthetic pauses and the product proves itself. A real browser
session selects eight local files, uploads bytes and waits for an authoritative
receipt. No hidden “play demo” button.

**VO:** Beat 2 from `SCRIPT.md`.

**Visual:** Real `browser-demo.webm` in a 430×932 phone frame at center-right.
At left, four factual rails draw in: Firebase Auth, App Check, Cloud Storage,
Firestore. A counter types from zero to eight as the receipt arrives. Fine SVG
lines terminate at the current phone element, never at arbitrary stars.

**Techniques:** video compositing; SVG path drawing; counter/typing effect;
velocity-matched lower-third transitions.

**Transition:** receipt brightness becomes a horizontal white scan line and
reveals the architecture chain.

**Depth:** BG dark field; MG service rails; FG real phone recording.

**SFX:** upload ticks, one restrained confirmation chime.

## Beat 3 — Deterministic first (75.52–113.55s)

**Concept:** Show cost control as a clear execution path, not a cloud-logo wall.
Each object passes through deterministic gates; only one receipt continues to a
paid processor.

**VO:** Beat 3 from `SCRIPT.md`.

**Visual:** Eight small fragment identities travel along a drawn path. Hash,
metadata, thumbnail and duplicate nodes light in sequence. Seven photo nodes
settle into `deterministic_only`; the receipt passes an Authoritative Routing
gate, then Cloud Tasks and Document AI. A tiny ledger shows “1 approved OCR / 8
originals”. No Gemini icon appears in this beat.

**Techniques:** SVG path drawing; GSAP MotionPath; character typing for route
reasons; deterministic Canvas particles around the selected receipt.

**Transition:** path curves downward and becomes the orbit line of the World
globe.

**Depth:** BG architecture grid; MG path and gates; FG selected receipt.

**SFX:** quiet routing clicks; deeper single pulse when OCR is approved.

## Beat 4 — One world, changed by data (113.55–149.74s)

**Concept:** Return to the real product. The same eight imported objects become
World and Bangkok spatial structures. The point is continuity: no new particle
wallpaper is loaded between routes.

**VO:** Beat 4 from `SCRIPT.md`.

**Visual:** Phone recording shows World, then Bangkok. A side label changes from
“1 city · 8 fragments” to cluster names. The accepted World and City screenshots
briefly freeze at full clarity while their semantic anchor points pulse. The
camera keeps drifting through one shared black space.

**Techniques:** video compositing; CSS 3D phone rotation; SVG anchor pulses;
velocity-matched zoom between World and City.

**Transition:** Bangkok originals move toward camera and become Field nodes.

**Depth:** BG shared point field; MG phone; FG live data labels.

**SFX:** low orbital sweep, soft camera push.

## Beat 5 — Search, focus, source (149.74–186.68s)

**Concept:** The database behaves like space but remains accountable. Search is
visible as a physical reorganization, then one source is extracted without
losing where it came from.

**VO:** Beat 5 from `SCRIPT.md`.

**Visual:** Real Fragment Field footage fills the phone. Typing “Common Grounds”
draws relevant originals inward while remote nodes dim. The Lens opens and the
video pauses long enough to read original, time, place, processing trace and
Document AI excerpt. A thin provenance line traces backward to the receipt.

**Techniques:** video compositing; per-character search typing; SVG provenance
path; CSS 3D focus/depth separation.

**Transition:** Lens closes in reverse; its three source dots remain and expand
into dated evidence nodes.

**Depth:** BG de-emphasized Field; MG Lens; FG provenance annotations.

**SFX:** filtered search sweep, source extraction click.

## Beat 6 — A discovery grows (186.68–219.50s)

**Concept:** Make the inference order unavoidable. Three originals arrive first,
then relation, shared place and only then the sentence.

**VO:** Beat 6 from `SCRIPT.md`.

**Visual:** Accepted Discover Detail frame is reconstructed in stages over the
actual browser sequence: 12 OCT, 16 OCT, 19 OCT; curved SVG links; Common
Grounds; final title. Each reveal is captioned “原件 → 线索 → 关系 → 发现”. The
final title remains still for a full reading beat.

**Techniques:** per-word typography; SVG path drawing; CSS 3D evidence depth;
MotionPath relationship particles.

**Transition:** shared entity contracts into the Else Orb.

**Depth:** BG sparse relation particles; MG originals; FG final title.

**SFX:** three paper-soft arrivals, connected chime, one warm resolve.

## Beat 7 — Else answers with boundaries (219.50–252.79s)

**Concept:** Else is an interpretation layer over the current memory scope. The
answer is useful because it is sourced and bounded, not because it sounds human.

**VO:** Beat 7 from `SCRIPT.md`.

**Visual:** The same Orb moves into the Else sheet. The real question is entered;
the direct answer appears, followed by three source rows and the uncertainty
boundary. One source opens back into Fragment Lens. A small factual tag reads
“Gemini · source validation passed”.

**Techniques:** video compositing; character typing; source-row stagger; subtle
audio-reactive Orb breathing below five percent scale.

**Transition:** source rows become a clean Google service chain for final proof.

**Depth:** BG current discovery; MG answer sheet; FG source links.

**SFX:** quiet read pulse, warm found chime, paper extraction on source open.

## Beat 8 — Verified and available (252.79–278.14s)

**Concept:** End on evidence, then identity. The deployment and verified Google
path appear without pretending every future capability is complete.

**VO:** Beat 8 from `SCRIPT.md`.

**Visual:** Eight verified services appear as small monochrome nodes: Firebase
Auth, App Check, Firestore, Storage, Eventarc, Cloud Run, Cloud Tasks, Document
AI, then Gemini as the final sourced answer. Test counts and “Firebase Hosting ·
public judge build” type on. The public World capture settles behind the
Elsewhere wordmark and closing line.

**Techniques:** SVG service graph; character typing; counter reveal; slow CSS 3D
pullback to the public product.

**Transition:** resolve to black, hold 1.5s.

**Depth:** BG deployed product capture; MG service graph; FG wordmark/URL.

**SFX:** low resolving chord, one final orbital shimmer.
